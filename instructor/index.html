Suggested facilitation plan

This plan is designed for SK651 from 1:30–4:20 p.m. and can be adjusted live.

  • 1:30–1:40 — Introduce Assessment Lab and roles
  • 1:40–1:50 — Round 1 encounter
  • 1:50–1:56 — Immediate pair/brief room debrief
  • 1:56–1:59 — Switch/move
  • 1:59–2:09 — Round 2 encounter
  • 2:09–2:15 — Immediate debrief
  • 2:15–2:18 — Switch/move
  • 2:18–2:28 — Round 3 encounter
  • 2:28–2:34 — Immediate debrief
  • 2:34–2:49 — 15-minute break
  • 2:49–2:52 — Re-form/orient
  • 2:52–3:02 — Round 4 encounter
  • 3:02–3:08 — Immediate debrief
  • 3:08–3:25 — Whole-class comparison before reveal
  • 3:25–3:40 — Reveal the assessment approaches
  • 3:40–4:05 — Teach/compare the four approaches
  • 4:05–4:15 — Advanced-practice synthesis
  • 4:15–4:20 — Exit reflection

Movement

For a class of approximately 28 students working in pairs:

  • Students alternate assessor and client roles.
  • One row can move one seat between rounds to change partners.
  • The instructor announces the round shown on the classroom screen.
  • Students independently select that round and their assigned role.
  • After each encounter, students complete the immediate verbal debrief before moving.

Immediate debrief

  • Assessor: What did you learn that felt important?
  • Assessor: What influenced what you asked next?
  • Client: What did the assessor's questions make visible?
  • Client: What were you waiting for them to ask?
  • Both: What is still unknown?

Whole-class comparison before reveal

  • Who do you think you knew best? Why?
  • What kinds of information did you learn in each round?
  • What information stayed invisible?
  • What did you hesitate to ask?
  • What did you assume without asking?
  • What changed what you asked next?
  • What was on the client page that the assessor never discovered?

After this comparison, display the assessment reveal.
