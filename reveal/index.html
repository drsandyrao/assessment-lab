What were we doing?

What we ask shapes what we come to know.

Different assessment approaches direct attention toward different information. No single encounter can tell us everything about a person. The purpose of an assessment helps determine what we need to understand, what questions we ask, and what we may need to explore further.

The four approaches

  • Round 1 — Comprehensive psychosocial/biopsychosocial assessment
  • Round 2 — Suicide assessment
  • Round 3 — Interpersonal safety / trauma-sensitive assessment
  • Round 4 — Structural/intersectional assessment

Known, inferred, unknown

  • What did the person actually tell me?
  • What am I interpreting or inferring?
  • What remains unknown?
