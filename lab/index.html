Before you begin

  • All client scenarios are fictional.
  • You are never expected to disclose personal experiences.
  • When playing a client, use only the information provided in the scenario.
  • Do not use your own experiences of trauma, suicide, self-harm, violence, sexuality, discrimination or other personal matters in the role-play.
  • The goal is to practise asking, listening, following up, and noticing what different questions make visible.
